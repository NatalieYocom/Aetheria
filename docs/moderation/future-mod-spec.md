In the future, Automatic Voice Moderation will be implemented.
All voices will be transcripted to check for profanity and harrassment.
voice logs will be available for 10 minutes after the conversation, checked by an AI and then deleted.
If something bad was flagged, It will not be deleted and will be reported to moderators, with voice logs and transcript to look at.
The moderators will be able to delete the report or bring it to attention.
