## Public Avatar Guidelines/Rules
- NSFW content of any shape or form is not allowed. This means genitalia of any form is not allowed. Suggestive Avatars are allowed, but must be tagged accordingly.
- Racism, Propaganda, Drama, and Individual Harassment will not be tolerated on any avatar.
- Depictions of real world violence, mass violence, terrorism, or sensitive political figures is not allowed.
- Extreme visuals, Gore, extreme mutilation, or rapid flashing/strobe visuals designed to induce seizures or discomfort are not allowed.
- Avatars cannot contain any form of scripting/modeling designed for malicious intent, such as crashers, glitchers, etc.
- Avatars cannot spam loud audio.
- DMCA Takedowns will be followed.
